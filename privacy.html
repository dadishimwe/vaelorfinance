Privacy policy

This page explains what Vaelor Finance records when you use vaelorfinance.com. It was last updated on 7 October 2026.

Who we are

Vaelor Finance provides financial consulting and auditing from KG 7 Ave, Kigali Heights, Kigali, Rwanda. Questions about this policy can go to info@vaelorfinance.com or +250 785 912 584.

Cookies and site visits

The site asks before it measures visits. If you choose Reject, no analytics cookies are set. If you choose Accept, we use Google Analytics 4 to record:

  • pages you open
  • approximate location, such as country or city
  • how long a visit stays on a page
  • general device and browser information

Google processes that information for us. Their description of the service is at policies.google.com/privacy. You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings in the footer. Rejecting after an earlier acceptance takes effect on the next page.

Messages you send us

The contact form collects your name, email address, phone number, budget, and message so we can reply. That information is emailed to Vaelor Finance. We use it to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell it.

How long we keep it

Analytics data stays in Google Analytics according to that account’s retention settings. Contact messages stay in our email while we need them for the conversation and our records.

Your choices

Use Accept or Reject on the cookie banner, or open Cookie settings later. To ask about a message you sent, email info@vaelorfinance.com.
